01

VEX AI Robot Intelligence System

With Team 16610A, I helped program, debug, and improve autonomous robot behaviour using Python and Java. Our larger robot acted as a watchtower, using four cameras to build a field view and relay information to the smaller robot so it could choose its next task from current conditions. The work contributed to the team receiving the Robot Intelligence Award at the 2026 VEX AI World Championship at UC Berkeley.

02

Portable Printer Robot

Built a wheeled, two-axis printer robot with a partner for my school computer engineering club. The robot moved the paper along the y-axis while a track moved the pen along the x-axis. Our project earned first place in the club competition.

Autonomous Driving Logic Circuit

Created both a physical circuit using IC logic gates and LEDs and a digital version to model simplified autonomous-driving decisions. I supported the build with a research paper using outside sources, figures, and data to explain the circuit’s design.

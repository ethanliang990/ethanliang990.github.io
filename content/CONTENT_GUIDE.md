# Content guide

Every value in square brackets (`[LIKE THIS]`) is intentionally a placeholder.

## Write with evidence

Use this simple pattern for projects and experience entries:

1. **Context** — What problem or question were you working on?
2. **Contribution** — What did *you* personally build, research, organize, or improve?
3. **Outcome** — What happened, changed, or did you learn?

Avoid generic claims such as “passionate,” “hard working,” or “excellent leader” unless the next sentence shows evidence.

## Public-safety checklist

- Do not publish a home address, phone number, school schedule, or login credentials.
- Only list achievements, titles, and numbers you can explain truthfully.
- Use an email address you are comfortable receiving public messages at.
- Test every project, résumé, and social link before publishing.
